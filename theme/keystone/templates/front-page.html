About Keystone Comfort

The measurement visit is the product

Every engagement starts the same way: ninety minutes, room-by-room readings, static pressure at the air handler, and a written comparison of your options priced against your house's actual numbers. If the honest answer is a small fix, that's the answer you get.

  • Measure first. Recommendations start with the house, not a product catalog.
  • Explain the tradeoffs. You receive options in plain language, including when repair is the sensible choice.
  • Respect the building. Comfort work should preserve the plaster, trim, masonry, and character already there.
