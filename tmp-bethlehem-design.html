Questions We Hear Often
How fast can you get here?
We keep a crew in Southside and another in Saucon Valley. Most of Bethlehem is within 30 minutes.
Do you handle steam systems?
Yes — Bethlehem has thousands. We maintain, convert to hot water, or build hybrid systems that keep your radiators.
What about historic district permits?
We handle all City of Bethlehem mechanical permits and coordinate with the Historic Conservation Commission when needed.
Is a heat pump viable here?
Absolutely. Modern cold-climate heat pumps work down to -5°F. We pair them with gas backup for the worst nights.
