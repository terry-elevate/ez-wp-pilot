Color System Rules
Max 5 colors. High contrast required. Body text color separate from palette.
High Contrast (Good)
Navy + Blue + Gold + Tan + White — each distinguishable
Low Contrast (Bad)
Sage + Sage + Tan + Tan + Cream — can't tell them apart

Standard Build Prompts
Rebuild Existing Site
Use same brand colors/fonts. New sitemap. 750-1000 words/page (50-300 for About & Contact). Global header/footer. Keyword optimization from attached doc.
Build New Site (No Existing)
Specify primary/secondary/accent hex colors. Heading + body fonts. Light vs dark. Open vs sectioned. Target audience. Sitemap. 750-1000 words. Global header/footer.
