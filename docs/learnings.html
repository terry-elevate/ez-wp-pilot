Requirements

What came out of the meetings

Six requirements, straight from the working sessions. More than page generation — it's the whole operating model for running hundreds of WordPress sites.

  • Scale without sameness

    50+ location pages per site, each one locally relevant, substantial, and visibly distinct when reviewed side by side.

  • Brand-true design

    Keep the brand's colors, typography, imagery, navigation, and footer standards — while still allowing layout variety across industries and locations.

  • Native WordPress delivery

    Editable Gutenberg content, structured sections, valid blocks, SEO metadata, internal links, and per-site fields. No Elementor dependency.

  • Repeatable quality controls

    Automatic checks on content depth, imagery, readability, uniqueness, metadata, block validity, and unsupported business claims — before anything publishes.

  • Fleet operations

    Central visibility and repeatable maintenance across a 300+ site portfolio: plugin inventory, bulk updates, one dashboard.

  • Controlled AI and deployment

    Agents work with WordPress through governed tools, inference can run privately on local hardware, and the whole thing runs self-hosted behind a VPN.
