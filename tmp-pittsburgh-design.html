Why Pittsburgh Homes Need Specialized HVAC
Pittsburgh's unique housing stock — from Victorian-era rowhouses in Lawrenceville to mid-century splits in Mt. Lebanon — demands heating solutions that respect both architecture and efficiency. Standard one-size approaches fail where ductwork meets century-old plaster.
The Steel City's climate swings from humid summers to brutal January lows, stressing equipment that wasn't designed for this range. We size and configure for Pittsburgh-specific conditions.
