BENVENUTI
A family tradition,
three generations strong.
What started as Nonna Rosa's kitchen in 1946 has grown into Lancaster's most beloved Italian dining experience. Every dish carries the soul of our family recipes.
We source ingredients from local farms and import specialty items directly from Italy. Your table is set with the same care we'd give our own family.
